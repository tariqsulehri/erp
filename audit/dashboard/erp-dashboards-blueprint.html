What to keep from the reference, and what to change

These are the habits that make the reference tool readable at a glance. Each one is carried into ERP.

A definition under every titleOne plain line explains the metric. ERP's scores are formula-driven, so this matters most here.
"View as table" everywhereEvery chart has an accessible data alternative. This is built into the ChartCard.
Answer firstA greeting with status chips, then KPI tiles, then detail charts, then tables.
Named chaptersShort accent-bar section headers group a long scroll into chapters.
Targets in the chartA dashed target line, a tick on the bar, a shaded below-target zone, and on/below-target labels.
A consistent data voiceMono numerals, counts above columns, % pills inside them, and end labels on ordinal axes.
Shape and letter, not color aloneLetter-coded cells and hollow pending markers.
Statistics with distributionsMean, median, P25, P75, P90, standard deviation and N under the strip plot.
A scope switcherDepartment / My team / Me, driven by role and access scope.
Change: grades use one ordered rampGrades are ordinal, so one blue goes light to dark. Unrelated hues per grade read as unrelated categories.
Change: no two-slice donutsUse a stat tile or a 100% bar, per ERP STANDARDS §8. Donuts are only for 3–5 part outcomes.
Change: darker solid tilesWhite text needs at least 4.5:1, so tiles use the darker Prism fills shown in the visual spec.

  • Every endpoint is scoped server-side through accessScopeService, validated with zod, cached for 60 seconds per (user, scope, period), and covered by the RBAC matrix test.
  • Privacy: any group aggregate below 3 people returns "Fewer than 3 people". Engineers only get scope=me. No individual values are ever returned outside the caller's access scope.

Visual spec

These are additions to the ERP Prism tokens in audit §10.7. Each tile shows its fill and the contrast of white text at the lightest stop.

Grade ramp (ordinal, one hue)

ComponentKey rules
ChartCardTitle 16/600, a plain-language definition line, then the chart, then a footer with "View as table" and Export. Radius 12, 20 px padding.
KpiTileSolid tile fill, icon chip top-right, value in JetBrains Mono 600 32 px tabular, caption at 85% white, optional status chip, sparkline and delta. The delta uses an arrow and text, never color alone.
CapsuleColumnsFull-height track, fill from the bottom, count above, % pill inside the fill, ordinal end labels
BarList / BulletBarTruncated label, 8–9 px track, value right-aligned in mono. Risky rows use the serious color plus ⚠. Bullets have a 2 px target tick.
StripPlot8 px dots stacked when values collide, dashed target, shaded below-target zone, statistics row computed by a tested pure function
StatusGridLetter-coded cells, hollow when pending, sticky first column, roving-tabindex keyboard navigation
UnitMatrix / SegmentedBarFour shape-distinct states, 2 px gaps between segments, counts as big mono numbers
OrgChartCards with initials avatar, grade pill on the ramp, "N reports", mini pillar ring and pending badge. Collapse and search. Scrolls inside its own container.
CalendarMonthToday ringed, weekends muted, milestones solid, pending dashed, "+N more" popover, Calendar/List toggle
AvatarA deterministic light tint per department with dark text at 4.5:1 or better. Never white on mid-tones.

Page layouts

Four pages carry the new widgets. Each section follows the answer-first order.

Home

Everyone · replaces today's Overview · D2
  1. Greeting hero with status chips (each chip links to its screen)
  2. Scope switcher: Department, My team, Me
  3. At a glance: 8 KPI tiles (4 / 2 / 1 columns)
  4. Appraisal progress: period hero, unit matrix, status mix, review velocity
  5. Attainment: distribution, strip plot, largest gaps
  6. Teams: teams against target, per-manager table
  7. Me: pillar triad, readiness checklist, my assessments

Workforce

Leaders, Admin · new route /app/workforce · D3
  1. Structure: org chart, span of control, people-managers stat
  2. Headcount by grade with readiness overlay
  3. Tenure distribution ("Unknown" kept as its own column)
  4. Progression: time at current grade, ready but not promoted

Skills matrix

Line managers, Leaders · upgrade of the competency matrix report · D4
  1. Filters: department, grade, pillar
  2. People × domains status grid, or people × pillars
  3. View as table and CSV export

Schedule

Line managers, Leaders · D4
  1. Appraisal calendar with a Calendar/List toggle
  2. Review velocity: submitted vs approved per week
  3. Approval outcomes (approved, pending, returned), linked to Approvals

Roadmap

The D-phases slot into the master plan:

  • D0 runs inside engineering P2.
  • D1 follows the chart system (UI-4).
  • D2–D4 replace UI-5.
  • D5 waits for the appraisal-history fix.
  • D6 merges into UI-8.

Decisions needed

  • Smallest group size for aggregates. The default is 3. Below that, a chart shows "Fewer than 3 people" instead of a figure.
  • Joining dates and grade history. Collect them now, either by HR import or manual entry? Until then, backfilled history shows "since unknown" and tenure shows "Unknown".
  • Home for engineers. Should the Me view also show anonymized department context, such as "your attainment vs the department median"? Only allowed if groups stay at or above the minimum size.
